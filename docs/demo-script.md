# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

| Time | Screen | Say and do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing | "Genesis Twin is a 3D digital twin of the body for research and education." Point out the disclaimer. |
| 0:20 | Public explorer | Rotate the avatar, switch male and female. |
| 0:40 | Explorer, layers | Toggle systems, change opacity, click an organ and show its vitals panel. |
| 1:00 | Explorer, sliders | Move sleep, exercise, smoking and age. Organs change colour and vitals update. |
| 1:30 | Pricing | Show Free vs Professional, then the test-mode checkout and log in as the pro user. |
| 1:50 | Pro dashboard, Pathogen lab | Pick a virus, drag the day slider, raise the treatment slider. |
| 2:15 | Pathogen lab upload | Upload the sample CSV and show the fitted result with uncertainty. |
| 2:35 | Research feed | Papers used this week, preprints flagged, model version history. |
| 2:55 | Close | Mention admin approval gate and the research and education label. |

## Prepare before the demo
- [ ] Backend running (docker compose up) and test-mode checkout works, or mock checkout ready
- [ ] Pro test account: pro@example.com (agree password with Rakhi)
- [ ] Sample CSV for pathogen upload (columns: day, load)
- [ ] Backup video recorded and on the laptop and phone
- [ ] Screenshots of every screen saved in case the live demo fails
