# Consent screen text

Shown before any upload of body measurements or CT/MRI scans (Personal twin) or pathogen data (Pathogen lab).

## Title
Before you upload data

## Body
Genesis Twin builds a simulated digital twin from the data you provide. Please read and agree to each point.

- [ ] **Research and education only.** Results are simulations, not medical advice or diagnosis.
- [ ] **Anonymised data only.** I confirm that my upload contains no name, date of birth, patient ID, address or other identifying details. Scans must be anonymised before upload.
- [ ] **I have the right to upload this data.** It is my own data, public sample data, or I have explicit consent from the person it belongs to.
- [ ] **How data is handled.** Files are stored encrypted, used only to build your twin or run your experiment, and not shared with other users.
- [ ] **My control.** I can delete all my uploaded data and experiments at any time with the "Delete my data" button in Billing and Account.

## Buttons
- Primary: **I agree and continue** (disabled until every box is ticked)
- Secondary: **Cancel**

## Delete-my-data confirmation
"This will permanently delete your uploads, personal twin and saved experiments. This cannot be undone." Buttons: **Delete everything** / **Keep my data**
