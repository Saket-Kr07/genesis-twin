# Research and education disclaimer

**Short form (footer and banner on every screen):**
Genesis Twin is a research and education simulation. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose, treat or make decisions about health.

**Long form (landing page, consent screen, PDF reports):**
All values shown in Genesis Twin, including vitals, organ health scores, pathogen curves and predictions, are outputs of simulation models. They are not measurements of a real person. Results come with uncertainty ranges and some are low confidence. Models are trained on public datasets and published research and may be wrong. Weekly model updates are validated and approved by an administrator, and every source is listed in the Sources Used panel. Preprints are not peer reviewed. If you have a health concern, speak to a qualified medical professional.
